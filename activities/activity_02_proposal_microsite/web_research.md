# Craft
> Your placeholders are filled. Now deepen the prompt — add the specific objection handling and proof that makes this proposal win the room.

## scenario

You have a filled prompt from Stack. It has the right numbers. Now it needs the right arguments.

A proposal microsite is not a data report — it is a persuasion document. Each stakeholder has a specific concern they need answered before they sign. Your prompt must tell the AI exactly what argument to make for each person, not just what section to include.

This phase adds three layers to your prompt before you fire it.

## context

Look at your filled prompt from Stack. Find these three gaps and add the missing instructions now.

**Gap 1 — Robert Chen's objection.** His failed ERP implementation is the biggest risk to this deal. The prompt needs to name it directly. Add this line inside Section 2:

`Robert Chen's section must open with: "We understand your previous implementation challenge. Here is specifically what we do differently." Then list 3 concrete differentiators — not generic claims.`

**Gap 2 — Sarah Whitfield's relief timeline.** She spends 40% of her time on exception handling — that is her specific pain, not a generic "operations" problem. Add this line inside Section 3:

`Sarah Whitfield's before/after table must have exactly these rows: Invoice processing time, Exception handling rate, Manual touchpoints per week, Time spent on reconciliation. Show current state → 6-month projected state for each row.`

**Gap 3 — The competitive guard.** The brief mentions competitive context. Add a closing section to your prompt:

`Add a final section: "Why Genpact vs [COMPETITOR → check competitors in the data file]." Two columns: Genpact strengths (3 points) vs key competitor gap (3 points). Keep it factual, not aggressive.`

## do_this

Add all three gaps to your prompt. The result should be 300–350 words total — a proposal prompt needs more specificity than a dashboard prompt.

Read the completed prompt once before moving to Validate. If you can point to every argument in the prompt and say which stakeholder it is designed to address, you are ready.
