# Frame
> Decide what you're building before you write a single prompt line. 5 minutes.

## context

You have read the data. Now make three decisions. Write your answers down — you will use them to fill the two blank areas in the prompt scaffold in the next phase. There is no template to fill here. These are your judgements about this specific deal and this specific CFO.

## do_this

Answer these three questions in your own words. 2–3 sentences each is enough.

**1. What is the single most important thing this microsite must show?**
Not "everything about Genpact" — pick one. What is the number, proof point, or message that makes Robert Chen stop scrolling? What do you need him to believe before he sees anything else?

**2. Who is in the room, and what decision must they make before they leave?**
Name the stakeholders. Name the specific decision — not "approve the proposal" but the actual commitment. What happens to the deal if this meeting ends without that decision?

**3. What would you cut if you only had 10 minutes to build?**
Which sections are essential to closing this deal, and which are supporting detail? If Robert Chen only reads one section, which one must it be?

Write your answers before moving to Prompt. You will use them in Area 1 (audience and decision) and Area 4 (what to build).
