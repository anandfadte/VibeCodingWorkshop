# Frame
> Decide what you're building before you write a single prompt line. 5 minutes.

## context

You have read the data. Now make three decisions. Write your answers down — you will use them to fill the two blank areas in the prompt scaffold in the next phase. There is no template to fill here. These are your judgements about this specific business situation.

## do_this

Answer these three questions in your own words. 2–3 sentences each is enough.

**1. What is the single most important thing this dashboard must show?**
Not "all the KPIs" — pick one. What is the number or story that justifies this QBR happening tomorrow? If a client only looked at this dashboard for 30 seconds, what must they have seen?

**2. Who is in the room, and what decision must they make before they leave?**
Name the audience specifically. Name the decision. If the QBR ends without that decision being made, what goes wrong for Meridian?

**3. What would you cut if you only had 10 minutes to build?**
Which sections are essential to the story, and which are nice-to-have? This reveals your priorities — and shapes how specific you are in the prompt.

Write your answers before moving to Prompt. You will use them in Area 1 (audience and decision) and Area 4 (what to build).
