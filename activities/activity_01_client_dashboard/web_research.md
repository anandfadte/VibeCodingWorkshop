# Craft
> Your placeholders are filled. Now deepen the prompt — add the specific insights that turn a data report into a recommendation.

## scenario

You have a filled prompt from Stack. It has the right numbers. Now it needs the right emphasis.

A QBR dashboard is not a data report. The leadership team needs to know what to act on. That means the prompt must tell the AI which numbers matter most, which signals are risks, and what the story of the quarter is — not just what the numbers are.

This phase adds three layers to your prompt before you fire it.

## context

Look at your filled prompt from Stack. Find these three gaps and add the missing instructions now.

**Gap 1 — Emphasis.** The revenue tile should visually stand out as the headline metric. Add this line to your HEADLINE SCORECARD section:

`Make the Revenue tile the visual hero — larger number, bolder treatment than the other three tiles.`

**Gap 2 — Risk narrative.** A red badge on a table row is not enough. The leadership team needs context, not just a flag. Add this line after your client risk table instruction:

`Below the client table, add a single-line alert callout: "Action required: [AT_RISK_CLIENT] shows [YOY_GROWTH]% YoY decline and High renewal risk. Recommend executive review before Q1 close." Use amber background, dark text.`

**Gap 3 — Pipeline confidence framing.** Coverage of [COVERAGE]x is a positive signal — the prompt should frame it that way. Update your PIPELINE line to read:

`Display pipeline coverage as a confidence indicator — green badge if above 1.0x. Add a label: "Healthy coverage — Q1 target is funded."`

## do_this

Add all three gaps to your prompt. The result should be 200–250 words total.

Read the completed prompt once before moving to Validate. If you can point to every element in the prompt and say where it came from in the data file, you are ready.

## checkpoint

Before moving to Validate:
- Emphasis instruction added to HEADLINE SCORECARD
- Risk narrative added with the action callout (not just a flag)
- Pipeline confidence framing updated
- Prompt is 200–250 words
- Every number in the prompt traces back to the data file
