# Brief
> Get oriented. 5 minutes. Screenshots show the visual bar — your job is to know the story before you write it.

## scenario

**Client:** Meridian Retail Group
**Deliverable:** Single-file interactive HTML QBR dashboard
**Audience:** Leadership team — quarterly business review, tomorrow
**Data files in your folder:** `meridian_q4_data.xlsx` · `genpact_logo.png` · `screenshot_1.png` · `screenshot_2.png`

## orient

Before you move to Craft, form three things. Each fills a named placeholder in your prompt — the AI reads all the numbers.

**Story** → fills `[QUARTER_STORY]` in your prompt
What happened this quarter? One sentence: good, bad, or mixed.

**Risk** → fills `[ALERT_ACTION]` in your prompt
Which client needs immediate attention, and what must the room decide before they leave?

**Hero** → fills `[HERO_RATIONALE]` in your prompt
Which scorecard metric dominates this audience — revenue, clients, NPS, or margin? Name it in one phrase.

## do_this

1. Open **screenshot_1.png** and **screenshot_2.png** — the design standard is set: Inter font, indigo `#4F46E5`, scorecard above fold, Alpine.js interactivity. Match it.
2. Open **meridian_q4_data.xlsx** — skim Revenue Summary and Client Risk Table. 3 minutes is enough.
3. Move to Craft only when you can fill Story, Risk, and Hero from memory.
