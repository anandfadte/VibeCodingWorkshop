# Craft
> Your placeholders are filled. Now deepen the prompt — add the specific insights that turn a data report into a Q4 budget decision.

## scenario

You have a filled prompt from Stack. It has the right numbers and the right channel highlights. Now it needs the right narrative — the insight that tells the CMO not just what happened, but what to change for Q4.

This phase adds three layers to your prompt before you fire it.

## context

Look at your filled prompt from Stack. Find these three gaps and add the missing instructions now.

**Gap 1 — The central tension.** The report shows 44.7% attainment with 95.8% budget utilisation — the worst possible combination. The CMO needs to understand this is a channel mix problem, not a budget problem. Add this line to the HERO SCORECARD section:

`Below the opportunity attainment tile, add a single-line callout: "95.8% of budget spent — this is a channel allocation problem, not a budget problem. Same spend, right mix = 150 opps."`

**Gap 2 — The reallocation math.** The worst channel (Webinars) spent $270,000 for 1 opportunity. The best (Executive Roundtables) delivers an opp for $12,917. This is a 20x efficiency gap. Add this instruction after the channel table:

`Below the channel table, add a Reallocation Insight card: "Reallocating Webinar budget ($270k) to Executive Roundtables would generate approximately [calculate: 270000 ÷ 12917] additional opportunities — almost closing the 83-opp gap to target. Recommendation: redirect 60% of Webinar and 30% of Paid Search budget to Roundtables for Q4."`

**Gap 3 — The funnel leak.** 1,847 leads produced only 342 MQLs (18.5% conversion). This is where volume is bleeding out. Add this instruction to the conversion funnel:

`In the conversion funnel, highlight the lead-to-MQL stage in amber. Add a tooltip or annotation: "18.5% lead-to-MQL rate — industry benchmark is 25–30%. Improving qualification criteria could yield 60–80 additional MQLs from the same lead volume."`

## do_this

Add all three gaps to your prompt. The result should be 300–350 words total.

Read the completed prompt once before moving to Validate. If you can point to the central tension, the reallocation math, and the funnel leak and say exactly what the CMO should decide from each one, you are ready.
