# Frame
> Decide what you're building before you write a single prompt line. 5 minutes.

## context

You have read the data. Now make three decisions. Write your answers down — you will use them to fill the two blank areas in the prompt scaffold in the next phase. There is no template to fill here. These are your judgements about what this budget meeting needs to see.

## do_this

Answer these three questions in your own words. 2–3 sentences each is enough.

**1. What is the single most important thing this report must show?**
Not "all the campaign metrics" — pick one. What is the data story that changes how the room thinks about Q4 budget? Is it the headline miss, the channel performance gap, the CPO difference, or something else?

**2. Who is in the room, and what decision must they make before they leave?**
Name the marketing leadership audience. Name the specific budget decision — which channels get more, which get cut, by how much. What happens to Q4 pipeline if the room leaves without deciding?

**3. What would you cut if you only had 10 minutes to build?**
Which sections drive the budget reallocation decision, and which are background? The channel performance comparison is probably essential — what else is non-negotiable?

Write your answers before moving to Prompt. You will use them in Area 1 (audience and decision) and Area 4 (what to build).
