# Frame
> Decide what you're building before you write a single prompt line. 5 minutes.

## context

You have read the data. Now make three decisions. Write your answers down — you will use them to fill the two blank areas in the prompt scaffold in the next phase. There is no template to fill here. These are your judgements about what VP leadership needs to see and decide.

## do_this

Answer these three questions in your own words. 2–3 sentences each is enough.

**1. What is the single most important thing this dashboard must show?**
Not "all 10 projects" — pick one. What is the signal in this portfolio that the VP must act on today? The revenue at risk figure, one of the Red projects, the RAG pattern overall — what is the opening?

**2. Who is in the room, and what decisions must they make before they leave?**
Name the VP audience and what authority they hold. Name at least two specific decisions that require VP-level sign-off — things that cannot be resolved below that level. What does the portfolio look like in January if those decisions are not made today?

**3. What would you cut if you only had 10 minutes to build?**
Which sections are essential for a decision-making review versus reporting for completeness? Red project escalation cards, revenue at risk, RAG summary — what is the minimum viable leadership dashboard?

Write your answers before moving to Prompt. You will use them in Area 1 (audience and decisions) and Area 4 (what to build).
