# Frame
> Decide what you're building before you write a single prompt line. 5 minutes.

## context

You have read the data. Now make three decisions. Write your answers down — you will use them to fill the two blank areas in the prompt scaffold in the next phase. There is no template to fill here. These are your judgements about what the CHRO needs to see and act on.

## do_this

Answer these three questions in your own words. 2–3 sentences each is enough.

**1. What is the single most important thing this dashboard must show?**
Not "all the HR metrics" — pick one. What is the signal in this data that the CHRO must act on this quarter? If this dashboard only showed one number, what would it be and why?

**2. Who is in the room, and what decision must they make before they leave?**
Name the leadership audience. Name the specific decision — a budget call, a programme approval, a headcount action. What does HR leadership need to commit to by the end of this review?

**3. What would you cut if you only had 10 minutes to build?**
Which metrics tell the story that drives the decision, and which are reporting for completeness? Start with the most urgent — attrition, performance, pipeline, or engagement — and build from there.

Write your answers before moving to Prompt. You will use them in Area 1 (audience and decision) and Area 4 (what to build).
