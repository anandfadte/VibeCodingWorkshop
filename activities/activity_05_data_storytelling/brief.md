# Brief
> Understand the scenario. 5 minutes. Know the campaign story before you build anything.

## scenario

**Campaign:** Genpact Enterprise Intelligence Suite launch — Q3 results
**What you're building:** Single-file interactive HTML campaign performance report
**Who will read it:** Marketing leadership and CMO — Q4 budget decision meeting
**Files in your folder:** `campaign_q3_results.xlsx` · `q4_budget_brief.pdf` · `genpact_logo.png` · `screenshot_1.png` · `screenshot_2.png`

The screenshots set the **design standard** — layout, colors, component style. Any numbers visible in the screenshots are illustrative. The actual data comes from the Excel and PDF files.

## do_this

1. Open `campaign_q3_results.xlsx` — look at Campaign Summary and Channel Performance. 3 minutes.
2. Skim `q4_budget_brief.pdf` — note the Q4 budget envelope and pre-committed channels. 1 minute.
3. Open `screenshot_1.png` and `screenshot_2.png` — note the layout and visual style.
4. When you can state the campaign verdict and the channel that should get more Q4 budget from memory, move to Frame.
