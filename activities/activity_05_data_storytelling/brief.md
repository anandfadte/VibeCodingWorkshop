# Brief
> Get oriented. 5 minutes. Screenshots show the visual bar — your job is to know the budget verdict before you write it.

## scenario

**Campaign:** Genpact Enterprise Intelligence Suite — Market Launch
**Deliverable:** Single-file interactive HTML campaign performance report
**Audience:** CMO, VP Demand Generation, and CFO — Q4 budget reallocation decision
**Data files in your folder:** `campaign_q3_results.xlsx` · `q4_budget_brief.pdf` · `genpact_logo.png` · `screenshot_1.png` · `screenshot_2.png`

## orient

Before you move to Craft, form three things. Each fills a named placeholder in your prompt — the AI reads all the numbers.

**Story** → fills `[CAMPAIGN_VERDICT]` in your prompt
Was this a failed campaign or a learning campaign? One nuanced sentence — not just "we missed target."

**Risk** → fills `[HIDDEN_INSIGHT]` in your prompt
Which channel looks productive on volume but fails on quality — and why does that matter for Q4?

**Hero** → fills `[Q4_BET]` in your prompt
Where does the Q4 budget go, and what opportunity number are you committing to?

## do_this

1. Open **screenshot_1.png** and **screenshot_2.png** — the design standard is set: Inter font, blue `#1D4ED8`, green best/red worst channel coding, CPO bar chart as the central visual. Match it.
2. Open **campaign_q3_results.xlsx** — skim Channel Performance sheet. 3 minutes is enough.
3. Move to Craft only when you can fill Story, Risk, and Hero from memory.
