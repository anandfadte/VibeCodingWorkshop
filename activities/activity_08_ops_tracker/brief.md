# Brief
> Get oriented. 5 minutes. Screenshots show the visual bar — your job is to know the escalation story before you write it.

## scenario

**Portfolio:** Genpact Client Delivery — APAC, December 2024
**Deliverable:** Single-file interactive HTML project portfolio dashboard
**Audience:** VP Client Delivery leadership review — Priya Nair and direct reports
**Data files in your folder:** `apac_portfolio_tracker.xlsx` · `escalation_matrix.pdf` · `genpact_logo.png` · `screenshot_1.png` · `screenshot_2.png`

## orient

Before you move to Craft, form three things. Each fills a named placeholder in your prompt — the AI reads all the numbers.

**Story** → fills `[REVIEW_NARRATIVE]` in your prompt
What is the management story of this portfolio in December 2024? Beyond "2 of 10 are Red" — what does the pattern mean?

**Risk** → fills `[ESCALATION_PRIORITY]` in your prompt
Which Red project needs a VP decision today — P003 or P010 — and what specifically must be decided?

**Hero** → fills `[P003_ACTION]` and `[P010_ACTION]` in your prompt
What action does each escalation card demand? Name the owner and the date for each.

## do_this

1. Open **screenshot_1.png** and **screenshot_2.png** — the design standard is set: Inter font, slate `#0F172A`, RAG filter defaulting to Red view, escalation cards above the project table. Match it.
2. Open **apac_portfolio_tracker.xlsx** — skim Escalation Cards sheet and Revenue at Risk sheet. 3 minutes is enough.
3. Move to Craft only when you can fill Story, Risk, and Hero from memory.
